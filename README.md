# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad
**Genre:** Crime, Drama, Thriller  
**Created by:** Vince Gilligan  
---
##  Overview
*Breaking Bad* is about a high school chemistry teacher named **Walter White** who turns to cooking meth after being diagnosed with cancer.  
He partners with his former student, *Jesse Pinkman*, and together they build an empire — but at a heavy cost.
> "I am the one who knocks!" – Walter White
